How Blood Donor Finder Works

From emergency broadcast to direct donor response in three simple steps.

📝

1. Register & Verify

Donors create profiles specifying their blood group and availability. Hospitals register and undergo administrative verification.

⚡

2. Instant Matching

When a hospital posts a normal or critical emergency request, our algorithm alerts compatible available donors in the vicinity.

🤝

3. Direct Coordination

Donors accept requests with one click. Hospital staff gets direct contact info, schedules the donation, and logs the life saved.
